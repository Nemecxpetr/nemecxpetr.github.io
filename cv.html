Statement

Petr Němec develops a sound-based practice at the intersection of composition, performance, installation, and participatory situations. As a sound artist, composition student, and performer, he explores porous relationships between the body, technical systems, and the environment.

His work is grounded in an interest in sound as both a material force and a relational medium — one that can activate collective listening, intensify the bodily experience of vibration, and open new ways of perceiving time, space, memory, and presence.

Focus: collective listening · site-specific sonic interventions · multichannel live electronics · sound installation · text / graphic scores · field recording · cross-modal convolution
