between performances: the water melts away and the screens stay mostly blank, the audience just draws. The shared glow stays on (at the strength below); pause and the progress don't apply. Switching it off starts every screen fresh.
  full when
every phone shows the glow of where both audiences touch, in their colours (own particles stay on the phone that drew them). full when: the share of a role's phones that must draw on one spot for its glow to (nearly) fill; lower = a few people light it up, higher = it takes the hall
  build-up
  leak back
  crowd curve
the crowd pressure pushes a role's progress the touch way: cosi colder, čímsi warmer (up to 0.4 × strength). Pressure builds while the role's phones are touched and leaks back when they stop. build-up: with the whole group touching, pressure rises by 1 ÷ build-up each second at first. leak back: how slowly it returns, in seconds. crowd curve: 1 = every phone counts the same, higher = a few phones count little and most of the hall a lot. A crowd that keeps touching settles at sharecurve × leak ÷ build-up.
